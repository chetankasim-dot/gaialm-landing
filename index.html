Questions

The short answers.

What GaiaLM is, how it sits in your stack, and how it stays defensible.

How is GaiaLM different from a general-purpose LLM?
A general-purpose model generates everything — including numbers — from probability. It will happily produce a plausible-looking emissions figure or rating that is simply invented, with no source and no way to reproduce it. GaiaLM inverts that: a deterministic engine computes every number from validated methods and your data, and the language model is confined to explaining those numbers. You get the fluency of an LLM with none of the fabrication risk, and a full audit trail behind every figure.
We already have an ESG data platform. Why do we need GaiaLM?
Those are systems of record — they store and organise your ESG data. GaiaLM is the system of intelligence that sits on top: it reasons over that data, runs the regulatory calculations, assesses gaps, models decisions and drafts defensible narrative. It ingests from your existing platform and feeds structured, sourced outputs back to it. The record keeps the data; the intelligence layer decides what it means.
Is GaiaLM a reporting platform?
Reporting platforms are the last mile — they format finished figures into filings and disclosures. GaiaLM works upstream, doing the calculation, assessment and analysis that produce those figures in the first place, each with provenance attached. Outputs export cleanly into whatever disclosure or reporting tool you already use, so it complements rather than replaces them.
How do you stop the model from inventing numbers?
The Anti-Fabrication Layer structurally separates computation from language. The model has no authority to produce a figure — it can only read and narrate what the deterministic engine has already computed. If the engine didn't calculate it, it cannot appear in the output. Every figure carries its inputs, method and source so it can be re-run and reconciled.
Which frameworks, ratings and sectors do you cover?
75+ workflows across climate risk, sustainable finance, carbon & trade compliance (CBAM, CCTS), nature (TNFD, EUDR, FLAG), targets (SBTi), corporate & product compliance (CSRD, CSDDD, BRSR, REACH, EPR), ratings readiness (EcoVadis, CDP, DJSI, MSCI, Sustainalytics) and gap assessors for every major standard. The engine is industry-agnostic, with methods calibrated per sector.
